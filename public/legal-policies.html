Last Updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mak Trade Links (operating under the commercial brand name MISVI, hereinafter referred to as "MISVI", "Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data obtained from visitors and consumers ("you" or "Data Principal") via our online store. This document is drafted in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules).

1. Data Collection & Categories of Personal Information

We only collect information strictly necessary for the fulfillment of orders, delivery of goods, customer support, and statutory tax compliance:

  • Identity and Contact Identifiers: Name, delivery address, billing address, active mobile number, and email address.
  • Transaction & Fulfillment Records: Order items, quantity, transaction amounts, invoice identifiers, and payment confirmation status.
  • Payment Card / UPI Security Safeguards: We DO NOT collect, process, or store raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or net banking passwords on our servers. All monetary transactions are processed securely through RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Cashfree Payments, Razorpay, or UPI rails) via end-to-end 256-bit TLS encrypted protocols in compliance with PCI-DSS guidelines.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and session data collected via strictly essential cookies to maintain shopping cart states and session stability.

2. Lawful Basis and Purposes of Processing

Your personal data is processed strictly under clear lawful grounds:

  • Order Fulfillment & Logistics: Generating tax invoices, packaging products, printing shipping labels, and handing packages over to verified courier partners.
  • Transactional Notifications: Dispatching real-time SMS, WhatsApp, and email alerts containing shipment tracking URLs and delivery OTPs.
  • Customer Service: Resolving queries, tracking return/replacement requests, and answering grievance communications.
  • Legal & Tax Compliance: Maintaining statutory sales ledgers and invoices as mandated under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act, 2017.

3. Third-Party Disclosures & Data Transfers

MISVI does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only to vetted third-party service providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements:

  • Logistics & Courier Aggregators: Third-party carriers (e.g., Shiprocket, Delhivery, Bluedart, India Post) to effect physical delivery.
  • Authorized Payment Gateways: To authorize and settle payments and execute refunds.
  • Statutory Authorities: When formally requested by judicial, tax, or law enforcement bodies pursuant to applicable Indian laws.

4. Rights of Data Principals under DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal, you possess statutory rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of personal data held about you and processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the update of inaccurate data or deletion of data no longer required for statutory or contractual purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for promotional communications at any time.
  • Right to Nominate: You may designate an individual to exercise your data rights in the event of incapacity or death.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You may raise data protection complaints directly with our designated Grievance Officer.

5. Cookie Policy & Security Standards

Our website utilizes functional cookies to keep track of items added to your shopping cart and remember session preferences. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, certain e-commerce checkout functions may be restricted. We implement industry-standard organizational and physical safeguards (ISO/IEC 27001 aligned measures) to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

At MISVI, we take utmost care to manufacture and dispatch BIS-certified, safe, and premium educational toys. However, in the rare event that an issue arises with your delivery, our policy guarantees transparent and swift resolution in accordance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Return & Replacement Eligibility Criteria

We provide a 7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement / Return Window commencing from the date of confirmed package delivery, applicable under the following circumstances:

  • Product received in physically damaged condition or with broken seals.
  • Manufacturing functional defect (e.g., electronic audio buttons, lights, or charging ports not operating).
  • Missing parts, accessories, charging cords, or cards specified on the packaging.
  • Incorrect product or color variant received compared to your placed order.

2. Mandatory Unboxing Documentation Guidelines

To protect genuine consumers and enable expedited courier damage claims:

  • Customers are advised to record a continuous, uncut unboxing video showing the outer shipping label, sealed package condition, opening process, and the specific defect.
  • Damage or transit tampering claims must be reported within 48 hours of delivery to our customer support team at care@misvi.in or WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210 with clear photographic and video proof.

3. Non-Returnable & Non-Refundable Items

The following items cannot be returned or replaced:

  • Items returned without original packaging, product boxes, MRP tags, or missing components/accessories.
  • Products showing physical damage, water ingress, or wear resulting from misuse, drops, or unauthorized alteration.
  • Claims submitted after the expiry of the 7-day delivery window.

4. Step-by-Step Return Process

  • Step 1: Contact customer support via email at care@misvi.in or WhatsApp with your Order ID, reason for return, and unboxing images/video.
  • Step 2: Our support team will review and approve the request within 24–48 hours and schedule a reverse pickup through our logistics partner.
  • Step 3: Securely package the product in its original box with all accessories for the courier pickup agent.
  • Step 4: Once received and verified at our fulfillment center, a replacement unit is dispatched within 24 hours, or a full refund is initiated.

5. Cancellation & Refund Processing Timelines

  • Order Cancellation: You may cancel an order free of charge any time prior to shipment dispatch. Once the parcel is handed over to the courier, standard return protocols apply upon delivery.
  • Refund Mode & Timelines: Approved refunds are credited back to the original source payment method (Credit/Debit Card, NetBanking, UPI, or Wallet) within 5 to 7 business days of inspection approval. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are credited to the customer's verified bank account or UPI ID.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

MISVI partners with leading national logistics providers (including Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, Xpressbees, and India Post) to ensure safe, timely delivery across all serviceable pin codes across India.

1. Dispatch and Transit Timelines

  • Order Processing & Dispatch: Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours from our primary fulfillment hub in Vadodara, Gujarat.
  • Metro & Tier-1 Cities: Estimated delivery within 2 to 4 business days post-dispatch.
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: Estimated delivery within 4 to 6 business days post-dispatch.
  • Northeast & Remote / Island Regions: Estimated delivery within 6 to 9 business days post-dispatch.

2. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery Threshold

  • Standard Shipping: A flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies to prepaid orders below ₹499.
  • Free Shipping: All orders exceeding ₹499 qualify for FREE PAN-India Delivery.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): A nominal handling fee of ₹40 applies on COD orders to cover reverse verification and cash collection logistics.

3. Real-Time Tracking & Delivery Verification

Upon order handover to our logistics partner, an automated confirmation message containing your airway bill (AWB) number and direct tracking URL is sent via SMS, WhatsApp, and email. Courier partners will require OTP verification or customer signature upon handover of high-value consignments.

4. Undelivered Shipments, Address Rectification & RTO

Courier partners will attempt delivery up to three (3) times. If a parcel cannot be delivered due to an incorrect phone number, wrong shipping address, or customer unavailability, the shipment will be marked Return to Origin (RTO). In such cases, our support team will reach out to verify credentials and arrange re-dispatch upon receipt at our hub.
